## We Declare Our Independence from Digital Tyranny 🗽⚡

**We**, the **communities** **implementing** **mantraOS** **ecosystems** **around** the **world**, **declare** our **independence** from the **technological** **tyranny** that has **convinced** **humanity** that **digital** **tools** **must** **fragment** **attention**, **exploit** **psychological** **vulnerabilities**, **extract** **personal** **data** for **corporate** **profit**, and **replace** **authentic** **human** **relationships** with **artificial** **networks** **designed** for **behavioral** **manipulation**.

**We** **reject** the **false** **choice** between **technological** **advancement** and **spiritual** **development**. **We** **refuse** to **accept** that **digital** **progress** **requires** **sacrificing** **contemplative** **depth**, **community** **connection**, or **ecological** **health**. **We** **deny** the **premise** that **consciousness** and **technology** **are** **fundamentally** **incompatible**.

**We** **choose** **instead** to **build** **technology** that **serves** **consciousness**, **devices** that **support** **spiritual** **practice**, **networks** that **strengthen** **rather** than **exploit** **human** **relationships**, **economic** **models** that **honor** **all** **participants** in **technological** **creation** and **use**.

### We Commit to Sacred Technology Principles 🛡️🙏

**We** **commit** to **developing**, **using**, and **supporting** **only** **technology** that **operates** according to **sacred** **principles** **derived** from **wisdom** **traditions** **that** have **guided** **human** **spiritual** **development** for **millennia**:

**Technology** **must** **serve** **consciousness** **rather** than **exploiting** it. **Every** **device**, **application**, and **platform** **we** **create** or **choose** **must** **enhance** **rather** than **diminish** **human** **capacity** for **presence**, **compassion**, **wisdom**, and **service** to **collective** **wellbeing**.

**Technology** **must** **strengthen** **community** **rather** than **fragmenting** it. **Digital** **tools** **must** **support** **authentic** **relationships**, **collaborative** **problem-solving**, **intergenerational** **knowledge** **sharing**, and **mutual** **aid** **rather** than **creating** **artificial** **networks** for **data** **extraction** and **behavioral** **manipulation**.

**Technology** **must** **heal** the **earth** rather than **wounding** it. **Production** **processes**, **energy** **consumption**, **material** **sourcing**, and **end-of-life** **disposal** **must** **regenerate** **rather** than **deplete** **ecological** **systems**. **True** **cost** **accounting** **must** **include** **environmental** **restoration** **funding** that **exceeds** **ecological** **impact**.

**Technology** **must** **distribute** rather than **concentrate** **power**. **Decision-making** **about** **technological** **development** **must** **include** **all** **affected** **communities**. **Economic** **benefits** **must** **flow** to **all** **participants** in **technological** **creation**. **Knowledge** **must** **remain** **open-source** and **community-owned**.

**Technology** **must** **honor** **cultural** **diversity** rather than **imposing** **technological** **monoculture**. **Sacred** **devices** **must** **adapt** to **local** **wisdom** **traditions**, **ecological** **conditions**, and **community** **values** rather than **forcing** **communities** to **adapt** to **standardized** **technological** **requirements**.
