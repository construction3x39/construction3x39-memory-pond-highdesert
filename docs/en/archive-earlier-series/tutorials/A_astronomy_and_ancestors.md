## ⭐ **The Stars as Teachers**

*Under the Rohini nakshatra (the "red one"), we learn that beauty and growth follow natural rhythms.*

### 🌌 **Vedic Stellar Wisdom**

Our ancestors knew something beautiful: the stars don't just shine down on us, they're part of us. Every element in your body except hydrogen was cooked in the heart of a star. You, sweet child, are literally made of starlight.

The Vedic tradition gives us 27 **nakshatras** (star mansions) that help us understand:
- **Timing**: When to plant, when to harvest, when to rest
- **Character**: How cosmic patterns influence personality  
- **Purpose**: What each soul came here to learn and give
- **Healing**: How celestial rhythms guide medicine and wellness

### 🏜️ **Desert Astronomy Practice**

Here in our high desert, with no city lights to blind us, we practice **sacred astronomy**:

**New Moon Nights**: Gathering to study the deep sky objects, learning constellations, and sharing ancestor stories

**Full Moon Circles**: Community celebrations where elders teach about lunar cycles and children share what they've learned

**Solstice & Equinox Ceremonies**: Marking the Earth's dance around the Sun with gratitude, reflection, and planning

**Planet Watching**: Following Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn as they move through the zodiac, learning their agricultural and spiritual significance

- Children learn to use telescopes and star charts
- Families track celestial events together  
- Elders share traditional sky knowledge
- Students document and code astronomical data

        constellation (find-constellation position)
        nakshatra (find-nakshatra position)]

     :date date
     :constellation constellation
