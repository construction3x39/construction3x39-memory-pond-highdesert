## Testing Your Brain Wallet Security 🧪🛡️

*"The wise find pleasure in water; the virtuous find pleasure in hills." - Confucius*

Before committing to your brain wallet, test it:

### **Strength Testing** 💪🔍
- **Length**: At least 20 characters (longer is better)
- **Complexity**: Mix of upper/lower case, numbers, symbols
- **Uniqueness**: Not found in any dictionary or common password lists
- **Personal meaning**: Connects to your spiritual/emotional life

### **Memory Testing** 🧠⚡
- Can you type it perfectly after not using it for 3 days?
- Can you remember it when you're tired or stressed?
- Can you reconstruct it from your memory palace story?
- Does typing it feel natural and automatic?

### **Security Testing** 🔐🧪
- Check it against "Have I Been Pwned" password database
- Ensure it's not similar to passwords you've used before
- Verify it meets requirements for all systems you'll use it on
- Test it works with 1Password and other systems
