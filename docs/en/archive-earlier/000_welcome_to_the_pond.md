## The Complete Journey Ahead 🛤️✨

*"Better to do your own dharma imperfectly than someone else's dharma perfectly." - Bhagavad Gita (Stephen Mitchell)*

This learning path will take you from having never touched a computer to building systems that could help care for whole planets someday. Every step is designed to honor both ancient wisdom and modern tools.

### **Phase 1: Sacred Foundations (000-019)**
We start with the very basics - getting your first computer, setting up accounts safely, and understanding what makes technology sacred rather than just complicated.

### **Phase 2: Building Understanding (020-039)**  
Learn how computers think, how data flows like water, and how programming languages are just different ways of having conversations with silicon teachers.

### **Phase 3: Hands-On Creation (040-059)**
Start building things! Websites, programs, and digital tools that serve your community and honor the earth.

### **Phase 4: Community Systems (060-079)**
Learn how networks connect us all, how to build systems that bring people together, and how technology can strengthen rather than divide communities.

### **Phase 5: Sacred Mastery (080-099)**
Advanced practices in AI partnership, blockchain for community governance, and preparing for the galactic future while staying rooted in earth wisdom.

## Your Sacred Setup Journey Starts Here 🛠️🙏

The very next step on this path will guide you through choosing and setting up your first computer - whether that's an Apple Silicon Mac or a Framework 16 laptop. We'll help you make this choice based on your family's needs, your learning goals, and your budget.

But before we dive into the technical stuff, let's remember something important: **every tool we use is sacred when we use it with intention to serve love.**
