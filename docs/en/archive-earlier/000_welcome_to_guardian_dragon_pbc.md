## What is a Guardian Dragon? 🐲✨

*"The sage stays behind, thus he is ahead. He is detached, thus at one with all." - Tao Te Ching (Stephen Mitchell)*

In the ancient traditions, dragons were never monsters to be slayed - they were wise protectors, keepers of treasure, guardians of sacred knowledge. Here in our high desert community, we're reclaiming that truth.

**Guardian Dragons** are the protectors of digital wisdom. They:
- **Guard sacred knowledge** instead of hoarding it
- **Share power** instead of concentrating it  
- **Build bridges** instead of burning them
- **Serve the community** instead of serving only themselves
- **Honor the ancestors** while preparing for the future

## The Taking Dragons (What We're Not) 🗡️⚠️

*"When people see some things as beautiful, other things become ugly." - Tao Te Ching (Stephen Mitchell)*

Unfortunately, our digital world is full of **Taking Dragons** - entities that:
- **Hoard data** instead of sharing wisdom
- **Extract value** instead of creating abundance  
- **Build walls** instead of building communities
- **Serve shareholders** instead of serving life
- **Exploit users** instead of empowering them

Taking Dragons include big tech companies that spy on families, social media platforms that divide communities, and any technology that makes the world smaller instead of bigger.

## Guardian Dragon PBC Mission 🌅🙏

*"Better to do your own dharma imperfectly than someone else's dharma perfectly." - Bhagavad Gita (Stephen Mitchell)*

As a **Public Benefit Corporation**, we're legally committed to serving the public good, not just profit. Our mission is threefold:

### 🛡️ **Protect Digital Sovereignty**
Teaching families how to own their data, control their devices, and make technology choices that serve their values instead of corporate interests.

### 🌱 **Nurture Technological Wisdom** 
Creating educational paths that bridge ancient wisdom and modern technology, ensuring our children grow up as creators, not just consumers.

### 🌍 **Build Regenerative Systems**
Developing technology that heals rather than harms, that connects rather than divides, that honors the earth while reaching for the stars.
