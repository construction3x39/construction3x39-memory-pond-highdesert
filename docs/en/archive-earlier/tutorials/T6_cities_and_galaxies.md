# T6: Cities and Galaxies

*"From neighborhoods to star systems, organizing at every scale"*

## What are Cities?

Cities are complex systems where millions of people live, work, and play together. They're like giant computers running programs for human civilization.

## How Cities Work

Cities are built from systems:
- **Transportation**: Roads, trains, bikes
- **Communication**: Internet, phones, mail
- **Resources**: Water, electricity, food
- **Governance**: Laws, services, coordination

## Cities as Code

We can think of cities like software:

```clojure
; A simple city model
(def city {:population 1000000
           :neighborhoods ["downtown" "suburbs" "industrial"]
           :services ["water" "power" "internet" "transport"]
           :governance {:mayor "elected"
                       :council "representatives"
                       :budget 500000000}})
```

## Scaling Up: Galactic Civilization

What if we could build cities not just on Earth, but across the galaxy?

### The Challenges
- **Distance**: Light takes years to travel between stars
- **Resources**: Each planet needs self-sufficiency
- **Coordination**: How do you govern across light-years?
- **Culture**: How do communities stay connected?

### The Solutions
- **Local autonomy**: Each world governs itself
- **Shared protocols**: Common standards for communication
- **Resilient networks**: Multiple paths for information
- **Cultural exchange**: Art, stories, and ideas that travel

## Building Galactic Infrastructure

```clojure
; A galactic civilization model
(def galaxy {:star-systems 1000

                            :latency "instantaneous"}
```

## The Patterns of Scale

Whether building a neighborhood or a galaxy:
- **Start small**: Begin with working pieces
- **Build trust**: Relationships are the foundation
- **Share resources**: Cooperation creates abundance
- **Plan for growth**: Design systems that can expand

## Technology and Wisdom

Building at galactic scale requires both:
- **Technology**: Tools to connect and coordinate
- **Wisdom**: Understanding how to live together

## The Path Forward

From our current cities to future galactic civilization:
1. **Learn from cities**: What works, what doesn't
2. **Design for resilience**: Systems that can handle problems
3. **Build with diversity**: Many different approaches
4. **Center on humans**: Technology serves people, not the reverse

**From cities to stars** - this is the way.
